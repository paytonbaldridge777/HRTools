Don't rely on your Workday inbox. Your inbox only shows time employees have submitted. Run the Review Time report to find hours that were entered but not submitted.

What to do

  1. Run the Review Time report for {periodRange}.
  2. Approve timesheets that are correct.
  3. If time is missing or wrong, send it back and contact the employee. Follow up to make sure it was fixed and resubmitted.

You are responsible for making sure your team's time is correct, including when an employee forgets to submit or submits incorrect time. Estimated time should never be entered.

Another way to approve

  1. Open the employee's Workday profile.
  2. Select Actions, then Time and Leave.
  3. Enter a date in {periodRange}.
  4. Review the hours, then click the blue Review button (bottom right) to approve.
Out of the office before {currentSupervisorDue}? Set up a delegate before you leave and confirm they have access to approve. Otherwise your employees may not be paid on time.

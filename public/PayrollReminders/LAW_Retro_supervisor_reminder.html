What to do

  1. Run the Review Time report for {retroRange}.
  2. Approve corrections that are correct.
  3. If a correction is wrong or unclear, send it back and contact the employee before the deadline.

Another way to approve

  1. Open the employee's Workday profile.
  2. Select Actions, then Time and Leave.
  3. Enter the date of the corrected time.
  4. Review the hours, then click the blue Review button (bottom right) to approve.
Out of the office before {retroSupervisorDue}? Set up a delegate before you leave and confirm they have access to approve.

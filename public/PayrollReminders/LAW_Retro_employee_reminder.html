What to do

If all of your time before {periodStartShort} is already correct and submitted, you don't need to do anything.

  1. In Workday, go to the day you worked.
  2. Add the missing hours or fix the incorrect entry.
  3. Click Submit so the correction goes to your supervisor.
  4. Let your supervisor know. They must approve it by {retroSupervisorDue}.
Working this pay period ({periodRange})?
Those hours are not corrections. Submit them on your current timesheet by {currentEmployeeDue}.
  • Up to 6 prior pay periods can be corrected.
  • Corrections submitted or approved after the deadline are paid on a later payday.

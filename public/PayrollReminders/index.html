💼 Payroll Reminders

Upload your Excel file to generate manager reminder emails for unsubmitted and unapproved hours

📝 What this tool does:

  1. Reads your Excel file with employee timesheet data
  2. Required columns: Worker, UIN, Manager Email, Unsubmitted Hours Worked, Unapproved Hours Worked
  3. Groups employees by Manager Email
  4. Generates one email per manager with their employees who need attention
  5. Includes unsubmitted hours section and/or unapproved hours section as needed
  6. Employees with both types of hours appear in both sections

Result: Professional email reminders for each manager about their employees' pending timesheet actions.
